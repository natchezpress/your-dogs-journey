*2026-10-20_160529_CT · version 1.0 set · built from `assets/backgrounds/backgrounds.json` on natchezpress/your-dogs-journey (main) · prompts from v4–v7*

## How to read it

- **File** is the WebP in the repo at `assets/backgrounds/`; the 2K PNG master of the same name is in Drive STAGING › DedicationWall_Backgrounds › `02_Final_2K`. Every 9:16 file is 1648×2944; Cream is a 2048×2048 seamless tile.
- **Window** is when the picker shows it. *always* = every day; *any date* = the Milestones row, shown year-round; date ranges are inclusive and may wrap past Dec. 31; Easter and Dog Mom/Dad are computed each year.
- **In Memory** = shown on the In Memory picker (celebratory ones are hidden; Dusk is that picker’s default).
- **Pick** is the Midjourney image number (1–4, reading order) that became the keeper; **job** is the Midjourney job id, so any master can be re-pulled from `https://cdn.midjourney.com/<job>/0_<pick−1>.png`. The Spots are photographs, not jobs.
- The app cuts the 4:5 feed and 1:1 card from each 9:16 master by trimming the top and bottom 15 percent; a *text shadow* flag means the sky is busy and the dedication gets a faint shadow behind the words.
